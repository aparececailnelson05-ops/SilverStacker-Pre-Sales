Quick start (staging first)
  1. Settings > Permalinks > "Post name".
  2. Install + activate the theme, then the plugin.
  3. Follow silver-stacker-portal/docs/DEPLOYMENT.md (private uploads rule, encryption key, cron, SMTP, 2FA).
  4. Silver Stacker > Settings: review every "Unconfirmed" item.
  5. Silver Stacker > Integrations: connect QuickBooks (sandbox first) and optionally Google Sheets.
